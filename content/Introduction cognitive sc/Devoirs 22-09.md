# Miller 2003
#### Psychology dominated by behaviourists originally
"The first revolution occurred [...] when a group of experimental psychologists, influenced by Pavlov and other physiologists, proposed to redefine psychology as the science of behavior"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=1&selection=90,12,105,37|p.1]]
-> Behavioural revolution
"by the mid-1950s it had become apparent that it could not succeed"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=2&selection=16,15,17,25|p.2]]
#### Reintroduction of the study of the mind (1950's)
"If scientific psychology were to succeed, mentalistic concepts would have to integrate and explain the behavioral data"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=2&selection=33,0,37,49|p.2]]
-> Cognitive 'counter'revolution
#### Artificial intelligence, linguistics and psychology converge
"Marvin Minsky and John McCarthy were inventing artificial intelligence, and Alan Newell and Herb Simon were using computers to simulate cognitive processes. Finally, Chomsky was single-handedly redefining linguistics"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=2&selection=185,0,215,37|p.2]]

Late 50's : "experimental psychology, theoretical linguistics, and the computer simulation of cognitive processes were all pieces from a larger whole and that the future would see a progressive elaboration and coordination of their shared concerns"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=3&selection=40,21,70,37|p.3]]

#### Cognitive science as we know it today : interdisciplinary
"six disciplines were involved: psychology, linguistics, neuroscience, computer science, anthropology and philosophy."[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Miller_2003.pdf#page=3&selection=188,0,196,28|p.3]] -> *Cognitive sciences*


# Chamak 2004

#### Sciences cognitives en France structurée autour de deux orientations (computationnelle vs biologique)
"Pour les uns, l’hypothèse de base est que la pensée opère selon un processus de traitement de l’information et les fonctions cognitives sont assimilées à des opérations logiques. Pour les autres, l’esprit procédant du fonctionnement cérébral, son étude relève principalement des neurosciences"[[content/Introduction cognitive sc/Articles/Chamak_2004.pdf#page=2&selection=54,78,80,32|p.2]]

#### Importance des choix économique/politique
